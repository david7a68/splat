I read all four screenshots. Here is the assessment, ordered by impact at the table.

**High priority, usefulness at the table**

1. **Overview lacks the numbers a DM asks for most.** Initiative, speed, passive perception, hit dice, senses, languages, conditions, and death saves are absent. The right half of the Overview is empty, so there is room. Add a second tile row: Initiative, Speed, Passive Perception, Hit Dice.
2. **Spells tab has no spell slots.** Rohan's list gives no slot count, spell save DC, or spell attack bonus. Casting time, range, and concentration are not shown per row. Add a slot tracker line above the list and group entries by level instead of one alphabetical list that mixes cantrips with level 1.
3. **Ability tiles emphasize the wrong number.** "STR 9 (-1)" puts the score large and the modifier in parentheses. Players roll with the modifier. Show the modifier large and the score small.
4. **Saves and skills do not mark proficiency.** Morana's Constitution and Charisma saves are proficient but look the same as the others. Add a dot or bold for proficient and expertise. Also capitalize names, align the modifier column consistently between the two lists, and split skills into two columns. The skills list runs past the bottom of the window with no scroll cue, so Stealth and Survival are hidden.
5. **Equipment does not surface combat data.** Weapons show no attack bonus or damage, and armor shows no AC. Equipped items read the same as carried items. Suggest a bold or icon marker for Equipped, group by Equipped versus Carried, hide "Qty 1", and drop zero-value coin entries so "49 gp" stands out.

**Medium priority, hierarchy and state**

6. **Four identical blue highlights compete.** Campaign, Party, the character tab, and the section tab all use the same filled highlight. The eye cannot tell the levels apart. Use a different style per level, for example underline for section tabs and a pill for character tabs.
7. **The header stack is too tall.** Seven rows and about a quarter of the window pass before any content. Merge the campaign breadcrumb into the Party row, and merge the "Fetched 23s ago" line with the Refresh button.
8. **Fetch state is unclear.** "Fetched 23s ago" is far from the Refresh button, and the button says "party" while the text implies one character. A relative time must tick, or it becomes wrong within a minute. Loading, error, and stale states are not visible in these captures, so I could not assess them.
9. **The calculation footnote sits in prime space.** Two grey lines separate abilities from saves on every Overview. Move it to the tab footer or to a hover on the superscript marker.
10. **"Rohan of Unc" looks truncated.** If this is a truncation, add an ellipsis and a tooltip with the full name. If it is the real name, ignore this point.

**Low priority, polish**

11. Filter inputs have no placeholder text and no clear button.
12. Tag capitalization is inconsistent: "Class spell" versus "race".
13. Expanded descriptions lose structure. "Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot." runs inline as one paragraph.
14. Grey helper text has low contrast on the dark background.
15. Consider a party summary view with HP, AC, passive perception, and initiative for all characters on one screen. That is the view a DM keeps open during play.

The tiles, the Read only label, the Open on D&D Beyond link, and the entry counts are clear and work well. I did not edit any files.
