# Iteration 1: Set up a campaign (group `setup`)

Scope: first launch and empty states, Settings, Campaign packages and entries, column resizing, navigation state, and the design gallery. 59 captures at 1200x800 and 1 at 960x640 (`settings__01-connect-form__960x640`).

Navigation state works well. Library search, the selected entry, the Party member and tab, the open encounter and the Campaign tab all come back after a trip to Settings. The remove-campaign dialog, the cookie steps, the connect states and the blocked `Open campaign` reason are also good.
