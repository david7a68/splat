# Helm of Teleportation

<!-- SRD page 226 -->

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)\
This helm has 3 charges. While wearing it, you can\
expend 1 charge to cast Teleport from it. The helm\
regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.\
