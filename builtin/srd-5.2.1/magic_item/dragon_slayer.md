# Dragon Slayer

<!-- SRD page 219 -->

Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Rare\
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls\
made with this magic weapon.\
The weapon deals an extra 3d6 damage of the\
weapon’s type if the target is a Dragon.\
