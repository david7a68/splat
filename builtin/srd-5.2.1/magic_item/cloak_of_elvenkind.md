# Cloak of Elvenkind

<!-- SRD page 215 -->

Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)\
While you wear this cloak, Wisdom (Perception)\
checks made to perceive you have Disadvantage,\
and you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth)\
checks.\
