# Elixir of Health

<!-- SRD page 220 -->

Potion, Rare\
When you drink this potion, you are cured of all\
magical contagions. In addition, the following con-\
ditions end on you: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed,\
and Poisoned.\
The clear, red liquid has tiny bubbles of light in it.\
