# Spellguard Shield

<!-- SRD page 244 -->

Armor (Shield), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)\
While holding this Shield, you have Advantage on\
saving throws against spells and other magical\
effects, and spell attack rolls have Disadvantage\
against you.\
