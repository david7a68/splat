# Scimitar of Speed

<!-- SRD page 243 -->

Weapon (Scimitar), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)\
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls\
made with this magic weapon. In addition, you can\
make one attack with it as a Bonus Action on each of\
your turns.\
