# Boots of Elvenkind

<!-- SRD page 213 -->

Wondrous Item, Uncommon\
While you wear these boots, your steps make no\
sound, regardless of the surface you are moving\
across. You also have Advantage on Dexterity\
(Stealth) checks.\
