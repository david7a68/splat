# Darkvision

<!-- SRD page 122 -->

Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer,\
Wizard)\
Casting Time: Action\
Range: Touch\
Components: V, S, M (a dried carrot)\
Duration: 8 hours\
For the duration, a willing creature you touch has\
Darkvision with a range of 150 feet.\
