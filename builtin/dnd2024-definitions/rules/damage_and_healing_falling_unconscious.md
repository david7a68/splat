# Falling Unconscious

<!-- SRD page 17 -->

If you reach 0 Hit Points and don’t die instantly, you\
have the Unconscious condition (see “Rules Glos-\
sary”) until you regain any Hit Points, and you now\
face making Death Saving Throws (see below).\
