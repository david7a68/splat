# Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor

<!-- SRD page 92 -->

If you wear Light, Medium, or Heavy armor and lack\
training with it, you have Disadvantage on any D20\
Test that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you\
can’t cast spells.\
