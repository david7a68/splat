# Slippery Ice

<!-- SRD page 195 -->

Slippery ice is Difficult Terrain. A creature that\
moves onto slippery ice for the first time on a turn\
or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 10\
Dexterity saving throw or have the Prone condition.\
