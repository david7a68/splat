# Heavy Precipitation

<!-- SRD page 195 -->

Everything within an area of heavy rain or heavy\
snowfall is Lightly Obscured, and creatures in the\
area have Disadvantage on all Wisdom (Perception)\
checks. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames.\
