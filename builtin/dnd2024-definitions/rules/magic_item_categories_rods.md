# Rods

<!-- SRD page 204 -->

An item in the Rod category is a scepter usually\
made of metal, wood, or bone. A typical rod weighs\
2 to 5 pounds.\
Unless its description notes otherwise, a rod can\
be used as an Arcane Focus.\
