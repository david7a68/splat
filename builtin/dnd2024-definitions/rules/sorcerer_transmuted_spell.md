<!-- SRD page 67 -->

Cost: 1 Sorcery Point\

from the following list, you can spend 1 Sorcery\
Point to change that damage type to one of the\
other listed types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poi-\
son, Thunder.\
