# Damage Types

<!-- SRD page 16 -->

Each instance of damage has a type, like Fire\
or Slashing. Damage types are listed in “Rules\

<!-- SRD page 17 -->

Glossary” and have no rules of their own, but other\
rules, such as Resistance, rely on damage types.\
