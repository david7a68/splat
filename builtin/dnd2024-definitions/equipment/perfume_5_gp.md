# Perfume (5 GP)

<!-- SRD page 99 -->

Perfume comes in a 4-ounce vial. For 1 hour after\
applying Perfume to yourself, you have Advantage\
on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to influence\
an Indifferent Humanoid within 5 feet of yourself.\
