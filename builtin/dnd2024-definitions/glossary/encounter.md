# Encounter

<!-- SRD page 181 -->

An encounter is a scene in an adventure that is part\
of at least one of the game’s three pillars: social in-\
teraction, exploration, or combat. See also “Playing\
the Game” (“Social Interaction,” “Exploration,” and\
“Combat”).\
