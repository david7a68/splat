# Burrow Speed

<!-- SRD page 178 -->

A creature that has a Burrow Speed can use that\
speed to move through sand, earth, mud, or ice. The\
creature can’t burrow through solid rock unless the\
creature has a trait that allows it to do so. See also\
“Speed.”\
